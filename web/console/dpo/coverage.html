Bucket Definitions

Covered

At least one interactive consent record in the CMS ledger — any record whose verification mechanism is NOT a derived processing tag (e.g. WIX_MARKETING_CONSENT, an explicit form, a portal grant).

Derived-only

Consent ledger has entries but all are WIX_DERIVED processing records (a §6/§7 basis stamped by the derive-consents engine — appointment, order, form intake). No principal-given interactive opt-in exists.

Orphan

No consent record of any kind in the CMS for this data subject. The installation holds their data with zero legal basis on file. §6 DPDPA liability — requires immediate action.
